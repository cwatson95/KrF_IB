# Slice C: what beyond the reaction list limits a 0-D KrF model, and what the repo has already measured

Path keys: KM = /Users/cooper/coding/Agent/projects/Kinetic_Modeling, RB = KM/SPECK/REBUILD, PIC = /Users/cooper/coding/PIC.

## Bottom line
1. **The largest limits are not missing reactions.** Five things limit accuracy more than the reaction list: how deposited power becomes Kr*, Kr**, Kr⁺ and heat (the EEDF and deposition closure), the excimer loss side (the Kr₂F* three-body row with its Tg law, plus quenching by F₂, Kr and electrons), electron–F₂ chemistry and F₂ burn-up, the lasing fraction f_B set by B/C mixing, and how n_e is set together with recombination. Each has a measured effect somewhere in the repo, from tens of percent to three orders of magnitude.
2. **None of LIGHT v0's outputs has a measured counterpart in the repo.** The validation audit concludes gain is the quantity to validate on, because it is linear in the populations while laser energy is exponential in the gain margin (PIC/Validation/validation_report.tex:118-123), and g0/α does not depend on the pump calibration (validation_report.tex:436-447). Adding g0(t) and α(t) as outputs is cheap and needs no cavity.
3. **Only one in-repo measurement covers a buffer-free Kr/F₂ fill:** Mandl, Klimek & Salesky 1985, intrinsic efficiencies 9.3/11.2/9.2 % at 4/10/99.6 % Kr (PIC/Neon/NRL_REPLICATION.md:257-261). REBUILD gets the 99.6 %/10 % ratio as 0.27 against a measured 0.82, and halving K34 (LIGHT's G5) gives 0.75 (NRL_REPLICATION.md:505-511). Scoring LIGHT against it needs an e-beam route and extraction.
4. **Recombination coverage is partial and has never been checked against experiment.** REBUILD has 5 dissociative-recombination rows and 14 ion–ion rows. It has no three-body electron–ion recombination, no F+F+M and no F⁻ detachment. No afterglow experiment has been scored: rows B7 and B8 of the experiment matrix were never built (KM/SPECK/EXPERIMENTS.md:70-71).

## Recombination

**In REBUILD (krf mode, 174 rows).**
- Five electron–ion rows, all dissociative recombination: R11p (e + Kr₂⁺ → Kr* + Kr, 2.24e-7·Te^-½ = 1.39e-6 at 300 K, lumping Kannari's two branches 1.2e-6·(Te/300 K)^-0.55 → Kr* and 1.9e-7 → Kr** into the Kr* branch, RB/CHANGELOG.md:3071-3074), ADR (Ar₂⁺), AKI_DR (ArKr⁺ → Kr**), HK4 (NeKr⁺ → Kr**, Boichenko), R19 (Ne₂⁺, Chen).
- Fourteen ion–ion rows: K26 and K108 (Kr⁺ or Kr₂⁺ + F⁻ → KrF*) in the Flannery turnover form [a·N/(N²+1.7)]·scale (Kannari Sec. II.B, Petrova 2022), plus AKI_F/AKI_FA (ArKr⁺), AIIR1/2 (Ar⁺, Ar₂⁺), FMN1 (F₂⁺ + F⁻, 4.0e-8), NF1/NF2 and HK5 (Lee forms). All carry the Debye factor S = 1/(1+R_c/λ_D) (RB/MODULES/chemistry.py:37-59).

**Missing.** Three-body (collisional-radiative) electron–ion recombination; the Kr** branch of Kr₂⁺ recombination (lumped away); F+F+M (scorecard.py:66-67); F⁻ detachment (photodetachment exists only as absorber routing). The phantom-era plan names "three-body + dissociative recombination cooling" as needed for a self-consistent cold Te (PLAN:4163-4172). In pure Kr, atomic Kr⁺ must convert to Kr₂⁺ first: K15·N² is 1.5e8 s⁻¹ at 1 atm but 1.5e6 s⁻¹ at 0.1 atm (arith). At LIGHT's 0.1 atm / 1e16 corner, three-body recombination of atomic Kr⁺ could compete. Not assessed in the repo.

**How much recombination matters, per the repo.** No experiment: the Shiu–Biondi afterglow and F₂-attachment decay tests (EXPERIMENTS.md:70-71) were never built. Model-internal: ion–ion supplies 29 % of KrF* formation in REBUILD (RB/CHANGELOG.md:2011-2013), 40 % + 13 % at phantom's Aurora gain peak (PLAN:2945-2946), 44 % of ArF* in NRL's model (RB/CHANGELOG.md:2873-2874). DR is the harpoon fuel line: heating Te 0.5 → 3 eV cuts the Kr*/Kr** supply ×0.37 and F⁻ ×0.13 (1702-1707). Afterglow: n_e falls from 2.7e20 to 3.8e13 m⁻³ between 130 and 300 ns (2447-2449), "F2 attachment + recombination kill n_e within ~200 ns" (2528-2532). The screening-form spread (0.695 vs Morgan's 0.397, ×1.75) is named as an owner of the F⁻ ×0.19 miss (783-787, 2749-2753), and screening raised α ×0.23 → ×0.34 (2777-2778). Phantom: DR set n_e at the DE-AR point (PLAN:3966-3969), and with only Kr₂⁺ recombination as the electron sink at 4 % Kr, n_e ran away to 7.4e19 (4006-4008). The Argon_UI model predicts n_e at 1 µs = 3.1× the 300 K 1/(αt) because Te hangs at 0.236 eV (PLAN:437-442), a prediction rather than data.
