  [ok]    working / resolved.  Includes deliberate by-design and by-scope
          calls -- the disposition is stated in the note.
  [warn]  OPEN work remains.  The note leads with what is open.

Every row names a source: a PLAN entry, a Known-bad list, a module docstring,
README_LIMITS.md, or a test.  A status you cannot audit is not a status.

"Fully working" is defined operationally in section 2 and gated in section 5.
It is NOT "the code runs" -- pic3d already runs.  It is: the loop is
self-consistent in 3-D (fields <-> particles <-> collisions <-> boundaries),
and it reproduces published benchmarks nobody in this repo can tune.

WHY EACH ONE IS LOAD-BEARING -- i.e. what is physically WRONG without it:

  1. ELECTROSTATIC POISSON SOLVER.  THE BLOCKER; everything else waits on it.
     pic3d solves Maxwell (Yee FDTD) and nothing else, so there is no way to
     get the self-consistent space-charge field of a discharge.  Consequence:
     no sheath, no cathode fall, no ambipolar field, no breakdown.  And FDTD
     cannot substitute.  Its timestep is Courant-limited by the SPEED OF LIGHT
     on a cell that must resolve lambda_D, while an electrostatic run is
     limited only by omega_pe.  With dx = lambda_D the ratio is

         dt_ES / dt_EM = 0.2 c sqrt(3) / v_th        (independent of n_e)

     = 124x at Te = 4 eV, 248x at 1 eV, 601x at 0.17 eV (computed 2026-08-03).
     So running a discharge on the EM path costs two to three orders of
     magnitude in steps for no physics gained -- the radiation the FDTD is
     resolving is not what a discharge is doing.

  2. MOBILE MULTI-SPECIES PARTICLES.  Ions and anions are currently immobile
     charge smeared on the grid ("by scope: ns windows" -- true for the wave
     instrument, false for a discharge).  Consequence: no ion dynamics at all
     -- no ambipolar field, no sheath formation, no ion transit, no ion flux
     to a wall, and therefore nothing for module 5 to emit from.

  3. ION-NEUTRAL COLLISIONS.  With no ion MCC, ions free-fall through the gas.
     At 1 atm that is qualitatively wrong: collisional drift, not free fall,
     sets ion mobility, which sets sheath thickness AND the ion energy
     distribution striking the electrode.  Resonant charge exchange is the
     dominant channel and produces the low-energy peak that any real IEDF
     shows.

  4. ELECTRODES / CIRCUIT / SURFACES.  Particle walls exist (kill + deposit
     charge), but there is no applied potential, no external circuit, and no
     dielectric surface charging.  Consequence: nothing drives the discharge,
     and RF self-bias -- a first-order effect in any capacitive cell -- cannot
     appear.

  5. SECONDARY ELECTRON EMISSION.  Without the gamma process a discharge is
     not self-sustaining: the electron population decays once the seed is
     consumed, because the only electron source is volume ionization by
     electrons that are themselves being lost to the walls.  This module is
     what closes the discharge loop, and it is why the Turner benchmark
     (gate G5.1) is only claimable at M5.

  6. COULOMB COLLISIONS.  Electrons never relax among THEMSELVES; only
     electron-neutral collisions exist.  ARPAE's scope note calls this "valid
     at swarm n_e/N", and that is true today -- but it EXPIRES the moment the
     code makes its own plasma, because at avalanche densities e-e scattering
     is what pulls the EEDF tail toward Maxwellian and the tail is what sets
     ionization.

  7. IMPLICIT / ENERGY-CONSERVING MOVER.  Explicit-only means dx < lambda_D
     and omega_pe dt < 2 are hard requirements; section 3 shows the cell count
     that implies.  Consequence: a run is either resolved and unaffordable, or
     under-resolved and numerically self-heating (in 1-D the explicit solver
     reaches ~1e9 eV -- a pure artefact).  The EC mover converts an impossible
     run into an honestly-labelled SMOOTHED-SHEATH run.

  8. PARTICLE MANAGEMENT.  Weights are fixed and there is no merge/split.
     Consequence: an avalanche multiplies macro-particles until memory ends
     the run, while depleted cells fall to a handful of particles and their
     statistics collapse.  Any discharge that actually breaks down needs this
     to survive its own success.

  9. E-BEAM INJECTION + PREIONIZATION.  There is no way to inject beam
     electrons through a foil boundary with a spectrum, and no preionization
     seed.  Consequence: the 3-D code cannot be pointed at the e-beam-pumped
     device this whole project exists to model.

TWO STANDING WEAKNESSES (not missing modules, but they gate credibility):

  A. PERFORMANCE -- single-core numpy, ~50 ms/step at 1e6 cells + 3e4 macros,
     while a real 3-D case is 1e7-1e8 particles (section 3).  Needs cell
     sorting, an optional compiled path, domain decomposition.        [M8]
  B. 3-D VERIFICATION -- the 41 existing gates cover the EM instrument.  The
     kinetic ladder (MMS, Landau, Child-Langmuir, Bohm, Turner) does not
     exist in 3-D yet.                                        [M1-M5, sec 5]

ONE DELIBERATE NON-ITEM: a per-cell 26-species KrF* network in 3-D.  It is
unaffordable and it is not what this code is for -- the chemistry stays with
phantom11 (board row 18), which consumes the EEDF/rates/deposition profiles
the 3-D code produces.

   200^3     100    8.0e8          80160     223   h        45.4 GB

READ THESE TABLES HONESTLY.  Three consequences, and they set the whole plan:

  (a) A FULL-DENSITY (1 atm, n_e >~ 1e15 cm^-3) 3-D PIC OF A 3 cm GAP IS NOT
      REACHABLE, on this hardware or any hardware this project will have.  It
      is 1e13-1e14 cells.  This is the same wall README_LIMITS 2.1 documents
      in 1-D, cubed.  No milestone below pretends otherwise.

  (b) THE AFFORDABLE 3-D BOX IS ~64^3-96^3 CELLS AT 50 ppc, single core.  That
      is 64 Debye lengths across.  At n_e = 1e12 cm^-3 that is a 0.5 mm cube;
      at 1e10 it is 5 mm.  So the 3-D code's honest job is: SUB-MILLIMETRE
      VOLUMES, REDUCED DENSITY, RESOLVED PHYSICS -- the regime where 3-D
      geometry actually changes the answer (filamentation, sheath corners,
      electrode edges, beam-front structure), with the device-scale answer
      staying 0-D/1-D.  That is a real scientific niche, not a consolation.

  (c) THE IMPLICIT MOVER IS THE ONLY LEVER THAT MOVES THE WALL, and it moves
      it by a factor, not an order of magnitude:

         dx = 1 lambda_D  -> 7.7e13 cells / cm^3
         dx = 5 lambda_D  -> 6.2e11
         dx = 20 lambda_D -> 9.6e9
         dx = 40 lambda_D -> 1.2e9        (still 1000x too many)

      At dx = 40 lambda_D the EC mover keeps energy drift at -0.017 %/period
      (measured in 1-D) where the explicit loop blows up -- so it converts an
      impossible run into a SMOOTHED-SHEATH run, not a resolved one.  Report
      it that way, always.

Design consequence carried into section 4: the field solver must be chosen by
MODE, not hard-wired.  Electrostatic for discharges (dt from omega_pe, not
from the Courant condition on c), electromagnetic for wave coupling (already
built), Darwin as an optional middle for beam self-fields without radiation.


================================================================================
4.  THE PLAN -- MILESTONES
================================================================================

Each milestone states: what gets built, where, what gates it, what it unlocks.
No milestone is "done" until its gate passes under BOTH interpreters (the
PLAN (68) two-interpreter rule) and ARPAE/CHANGELOG.md carries the entry.

M1..M9 build missing modules 1..9 of section 2.1, IN THAT ORDER; M10 is the
GUI.  The ordering is forced at the front (no Poisson -> no sheath; no mobile
ions -> no sheath; no ion collisions -> the wrong sheath), which is why the
electrostatic core is M1 and the Turner benchmark cannot be claimed before M5.
Missing modules 6-8 (Coulomb, implicit mover, resampling) become necessary the
moment the code makes its own plasma rather than being handed one; module 9
(beam injection) is the device ask.  Board rows 16-17 -- performance and the
3-D verification suite -- run alongside from M1 onward, not at the end.

         2-D FFT in the periodic (x,y) plane reduces the 7-point stencil to

         it reuses numpy's FFT -- no new dependency.  The fully periodic case
         is the same transform with the k=0 mode zeroed (net-neutral
         required), exactly as pic2d/field.py does in 2-D.
         Keep a slow reference (sparse LU on a small grid) for the gate.

         over.  D1 requires the SCREENED Poisson equation

             div[ (1 + chi) grad phi ] = -rho/eps0 ,

         and the FFT-in-xy / tridiagonal-in-z solve does not apply directly.

         solve (an excellent preconditioner precisely because chi is smooth
         where the plasma is smooth).  Do not write a general multigrid.

8.  KNOWN-BAD -- DO NOT REINTRODUCE

From ARPAE/CHANGELOG.md (read it before touching pic3d):

  * DC-BEARING SOFT SOURCES.  Any injected current with a net time-integral
    leaves static charge no absorber removes; it masqueraded as 4-17 % "CPML
    reflection".  Sources must be DC-free.
  * (nu + i omega) IN THE DRUDE CONDUCTIVITY -- an e^{+iwt} term in an
    e^{-iwt} formula.  It kills the cutoff and inflated alpha 1.27x.  The
    convention is pinned and tested in pic3d/theory.py::sigma_ac.
  * OVERDENSE-MIRROR CLAIMS WITH A SUB-CELL SKIN.  Keep the evanescent skin
    >~1.5 cells (raise ppw) before quoting reflection physics.
  * RAW dTe WITHOUT THE ZERO-DRIVE CONTROL RUN.  Grid self-heating at demo
    particle counts moves Te at %-per-ns.  Every heating claim is
    (driven - control).

From the repo root CHANGELOG.md and PLAN, still binding here:

  * NEVER CALIBRATE TO A BENCHMARK.  Turner, XPDP1, LoKI-MC and the W-value
    SCORE this code.  If a gate misses, the miss is reported and the physics
    is investigated -- no fitting, ever (PLAN (26)).
  * PARITY IS NOT PHYSICS.  A green regression suite means the refactor was
    equivalent, never that a number is right.
  * THE TWO-INTERPRETER RULE (PLAN (68)).  pytest under /usr/bin/python3,
    AND a headless smoke under the Homebrew python3 (numpy >= 2.5, where
    float(ndim>0 array) is a hard TypeError that numpy 2.0 only warns about).
  * NO SILENT SCOPE REDUCTION.  If a preset is sized down to run in seconds,
    the tab says so.  Section 3's tables exist so nothing here can quietly
    pretend to full-density 3-D.

New, specific to this plan:

  * DO NOT LET THE EM PATH DRIFT.  M1 adds a mode switch to engine.py; the

  * DO NOT PROPOSE A PER-CELL 26-SPECIES NETWORK IN 3-D.  It is unaffordable

10.1  NRL ORESTES -- the system-level comparison
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Orestes is NRL's first-principles code for the e-beam-pumped KrF/ArF cell:
electron-beam deposition, non-equilibrium plasma chemistry (23 species, 116
reactions for Kr/Ar/F2), 1-D lasing amplification, and 3-D ASE radiation
transport.  It was adapted from KrF to ArF for the Electra programme.

VERDICT: this repo ALREADY has the Orestes architecture -- but not in this
package.  phantom11 (26 species) + unified/ase3d (3-D ASE) + unified/ebeam
(stopping-power deposition) is the Orestes analogue, and GUI_v2 is its front
end.  PIC_MCC_3D sits one layer BELOW Orestes: it prices kinetically the
electron behaviour that an Orestes-class code assumes.

  => THE GAP IS A COMPARISON, NOT A PIPELINE.  (Corrected 2026-08-03 after
     the owner's warning: "phantom was seriously flawed and would need
     careful examination before implementation."  The warning is correct and
     the repo documents it; the earlier wording here recommended feeding
     phantom11, which is unsafe.  See 10.1a.)

     A 26-species network per cell in 3-D stays out of scope (board row 18).
     What this package CAN do is price, kinetically and from first
     principles, the electron behaviour phantom11 currently ASSUMES -- and
     then SCORE those assumptions.  Read-only, one direction, no dependency.

10.1a  WHY NOT TO FEED phantom11 -- TWO OPEN DEFECTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feeding a better EEDF into a model whose OUTPUT accounting is broken does not
produce a better answer.  It produces a more credible-looking wrong answer,
with this package's first-principles provenance lending it authority it has
not earned.  That is a worse failure than not coupling at all, and it is the
specific risk the owner flagged.

The two defects are OPEN in the repo's own records, not speculation:

  (1) PLAN (70), 2026-07-31 -- "the engine's electron-heating -> light
      conversion is internally inconsistent by ~18x", and the pre-existing
      CO2 inverse-bremsstrahlung term takes the SAME path, so the PLAN (67)
      CO2 numbers inherit it.  The entry's own conclusion: "No hybrid-pumping
      efficiency claim can be made until that is resolved."  Status: OPEN.
      An EEDF handed in from here enters exactly that bookkeeping.

  (2) PLAN (72), 2026-07-31 -- the Kannari p->s manifold relaxation
      (K_PS_RELAX, gated by LIT_QUENCH) defaults OFF because 0.0 is what
      reproduces og-pyfiles bit-for-bit.  At 1 atm of argon that rate gives
      tau ~ 0.45 ns, an order of magnitude FASTER than the KrF* radiative
      lifetime, so the default holds the 5p/4p manifolds collisionally
      isolated -- "a parity artifact, not a physics choice."  Under the
      default the marginal conversion of bulk-deposited energy is 54.2%,
      which BREACHES the <=25% W-value bound; with Kannari on it is 1.7%.
      The baseline light output moves 3.4x on that flag alone, and modelled
      device efficiency 3.05% -> 0.89%.  The default is deliberately NOT
      flipped, because choosing it by which value lands nearer the measured
      ~7% would be steering by an anchor (forbidden, PLAN (26)).  Status:
      OWNER CALL, open.

  Corroborating history, same week: PLAN (70) also fixed two real Ar-mode
  wiring bugs in the engine (r156 stepwise ionisation manufacturing Ar+
  without depleting Ar*; r157 wired as superelastic de-excitation while
  carrying an ionisation suppressor, freezing 4p->4s by ~1e12).  And the root
  CHANGELOG's Known-bad records that the parity harness is STRUCTURALLY BLIND
  to Ar_g-weighted terms -- a sign error on r115 worth 40% of dArF survived
  there until a term-for-term comparison found it.  Parity green is not
  evidence of correctness (PLAN (26) policy 4).

  THE SAFE FORM, and it is still valuable:

    * EXPORT, do not inject.  Write the EEDF, per-process rate coefficients
      and deposition profile out as data with full provenance (this package
      already has params + checkpoint for exactly that).
    * COMPARE against the swarm tables phantom11 uses.  A disagreement is a
      finding about the tables -- reportable on its own, with no dependency
      on phantom11's downstream accounting.
    * Any actual injection stays BLOCKED until (1) is resolved and (2) is
      decided by the owner.  Until then this package must not be cited as
      improving any phantom11 efficiency number.
    * And per PLAN (26): even after that, kinetic numbers from here SCORE;
      they never steer.

10.3  VERIFICATION TECHNIQUES -- one gap CLOSED, two still open
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The PIC-MCC verification literature is more developed than this package's
gates were:

  * Parodi & Petronio, Phys. Plasmas 32, 013902 (2025), "Step-by-step
    verification of particle-in-cell Monte Carlo collision codes" -- a graded
    verification sequence for 2D-3V electrostatic PIC-MCC.
  * arXiv:2602.15130, "Code-Verification Techniques for PIC Simulations with
    DSMC Collisions" -- MMS applied to the FULL kinetic loop by manufacturing
    the distribution function and inverting its CDFs (deliberately NOT by
    adjusting particle weights, which would perturb the collision algorithm);
    scattering-angle CDF tests; collision-error isolation against analytic
    source terms; and the refinement relation N_p ~ h^-7 for collisional MMS.

  CLOSED 2026-08-03: SCATTERING-ANGLE DISTRIBUTION GATES (6 new tests).
  This package had NO test on the angular distributions at all.  That is
  exactly the hole the literature warns about: a wrong angular model still
  conserves energy exactly, still tallies correctly, still reproduces
  n sigma v, and still matches the ARPAE oracle -- every gate we had would
  stay green.  Now the empirical CDF of cos(chi) is checked against the
  analytic one for all three angular models (isotropic / screened /
  Surendra), the isotropic directions are checked uniform on the sphere, and
  the rotation machinery is checked to deflect by EXACTLY the sampled angle.

  STILL OPEN:
    * full-loop MMS -- section 4's G1.2 covers the Poisson operator only;
      the manufactured-distribution version tests the whole kinetic cycle;
    * collision-error isolation -- averaging collision outcomes against
      analytic source terms to separate collision bugs from field bugs.

10.4  COMMUNITY BENCHMARKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * TURNER ET AL. 2013 (Phys. Plasmas 20, 013507) -- the capacitive-discharge
    benchmark already named as the M5 gate: four cases with every physical
    and numerical parameter prescribed, five independently written codes
    statistically indistinguishable.  NEW AND USEFUL: an open reference
    implementation exists (github.com/lase-unb/ccp-benchmark), so the
    comparison data does not have to be re-derived from the paper.
  * LANDMARK -- the community benchmark project for low-temperature plasmas
    (Hall-thruster oriented, 2-D).
  * arXiv:2510.21261 -- a 2-D ExB coherent-structure benchmark with an
    explicit "lessons learned" on multi-code comparison.

10.5  CAPABILITIES PRODUCTION CODES HAVE THAT THIS ONE DOES NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ranked by relevance HERE, not by general importance:

  1. SELF-CONSISTENT NEUTRALS via DSMC coupling (PICLas; Pantera, Comput.
     Phys. Commun. 2025).  Our gas is static and uniform -- a stated [warn].
     A PIC-MCC-DSMC coupling is the principled fix, and it is what removes
     "the neutrals do not respond" from the caveat list.
  2. ENERGY-PRESERVING explicit PIC + MCC coupling (arXiv:2410.14859).
     Directly relevant to our split electron-ledger / gas-reservoir
     bookkeeping -- there is a published scheme for doing this exactly.
  3. PARTICLE MANAGEMENT CHANGES ANSWERS, not just cost ("PIC/MCC simulation
     of capacitively coupled discharges: effect of particle management and
     integration", Comput. Phys. Commun. 2016).  That RAISES the priority of
     M8 (merge/split) from a performance chore to a correctness item.
  4. Coulomb / binary collisions -- standard in every production code
     (PIConGPU binary collisions, Smilei).  = our M6.
  5. External-circuit coupling -- standard for capacitive discharges. = M4.
  6. COMPLEX GEOMETRY: non-orthogonal grids, cut cells, adaptive Cartesian
     meshes.  We are a rectangular box only, and a hibachi/foil geometry is
     not a box.
  7. AMR (WarpX), GPU / SIMD with fine-grain particle sorting (WarpX took the
     2022 Gordon Bell prize; PIConGPU).  = our M8 performance item.
  8. Field/tunnelling and collisional ionization (Smilei).  Out of scope at
     600 kV and 1 atm -- noted so the omission is deliberate, not forgotten.
