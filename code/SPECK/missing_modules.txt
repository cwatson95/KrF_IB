#                                                                              #
#  THE ORGANIZING QUESTION.  The bench will carry instruments: streak          #
#  camera, capacitance manometer + ion gauge, Rogowski coil + HV probe,        #
#  survey CCD spectrometer, the MTL-5 CO2 probe, a pyro energy head --         #
#  and later maybe a Thomson system.  An instrument measures a SIGNAL.         #
#  A predictive model earns its anchors only if it forward-models that         #
#  signal: state vector -> what the instrument records.  Today the engines     #
#  predict plasma STATE (n_e, Te, Tg, species, g0, P_out) superbly, and        #
#  predict almost no SIGNALS.  The phrase "synthetic diagnostic" appears       #
#  nowhere in the tree.                                                        #
#                                                                              #
#  POLICY (inherited, PLAN (26), non-negotiable): predictive-only.  A          #
#  diagnostic module's constants come from cited atomic data (NIST            #
#  A-values, published cross sections) and instrument DATASHEETS               #
#  (response curves, bandwidths, sweep speeds) -- never from fitting the       #
#  signal being predicted.  Anchors score; they never steer.  Any line         #
#  list or cross-section table added for these modules is consumed by the      #
#  row-by-row table walk (REBUILD Known-bad: never filter a source table       #
#  by species already in the network).                                         #

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 M1  THOMSON SCATTERING  (the named miss)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MEASURES  n_e and the EEDF itself: the spectrum S(k,w) of probe light
           scattered off electrons.  Amplitude -> n_e; shape -> f(v).
 HAVE      Fragments, no spectrum: light_unified/eedf_multiterm.py
           bulk_temperature() returns T_thomson, "what a Thomson-scattering
           Maxwellian fit returns" (a fit-bias estimator, the closest thing
           to a synthetic instrument in the tree); phantom11/sweeps/
           postdischarge.py is "Route 2 -- predict the post-discharge kT
           decay for Thomson scattering design"; and the Chow Te anchor IS
           a Thomson measurement (FIX-A4) with a stated non-Maxwellian
           systematic -- docs/two-region-eedf.md even admits the embedded
           _LOKI_EPS column was hand-keyed to it (an observable consumed as
           an input, exactly what a forward model would undo).
 BUILD     thomson.py: S(k,w) from the model EEDF.  Non-collective branch:
           spectrum = 1-D projection of f(v) onto k (reads the EEDF
           directly, including the Langdon super-Gaussian and beam tails
           from the KINETIC tier).  Collective branch: standard dielectric
           form factor (electron feature at w_pe, ion feature) -- needs Te,
           Ti(=Tg), Z, n_e, all already state.  Report alpha = 1/(k
           lambda_D), the predicted spectrum through a stated instrument
           function, AND the Maxwellian-fit temperature vs the true <eps>
           (quantifies FIX-A4's systematic instead of stating it).
 NOTES     Regime numbers (Te 2 eV): 532 nm at 90 deg -> alpha 0.06 at
           n_e 1e14 cm-3, 0.57 at 1e16 -> non-collective-to-transitional:
           the EEDF is directly visible.  A 10.6 um probe has k ~20x
           smaller -> alpha > 1 even at 1e14: the MTL-5 itself could do
           COLLECTIVE scattering.  Photon budget: sigma_T = 6.65e-25 cm2;
           ~100 mJ at 532 nm, L = 1 cm, dOmega = 0.01 sr collects
           ~1e3-1e4 photons/shot at 1e14 cm-3 -- viable gated, and worth
           printing per run so the lab knows when TS becomes affordable.
           WHY NOW even without hardware: it re-scores an EXISTING anchor
           (Chow) and is the direct experimental test of the KINETIC tier.

 M2  EMISSION SPECTRUM SYNTHESIS  (the spectrometer axis)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MEASURES  Wavelength-resolved emissivity: what the survey CCD spectrometer
           (new_item_list.txt MEASURING addenda) records, and the spectral
           axis any filtered camera needs.
 HAVE      No wavelength axis anywhere.  Output exists at exactly three
           fixed wavelengths (248 / 193 / 95 nm, phantom11/sim_0d.py);
           REBUILD collapses ALL non-laser light into one scalar ledger
           channel radiated_escape (ledger.py).  The seed to grow:
           Argon_UI/emission.py already does 4 NIR Ar I lines with NIST
           A-values + Voigt widths (vdW 20 MHz/torr + Doppler) + the Ar2*
           126 nm continuum.  "[TODO] 248 nm spectrum / lineshape" is
           plans.txt:2809's own admission; DE-AR asks for spectra matched
           to 15% over 215-500 nm.
 BUILD     spectrum.py: emissivity(lambda, t) [W/cm3/nm] assembled from
           (a) bound-bound lines of the tracked excited manifolds -- NIST
           line list as a cited data tier, statistical-weight splits of the
           lumped states exactly as Argon_UI/emission.py already does for
           Ar(4p); (b) the excimer bands: KrF B->X ~248 nm (the sum-rule
           width Delta-lambda ~2.8 nm already derived in ase3d), KrF C->A
           broad ~275 nm, Kr2F* broad blue ~400-450 nm, Ar2*/Kr2* second
           continua 126/147 nm, ArF 193 nm; (c) ff+fb electron continua
           (small at these Te -- compute once, report, likely dismissable);
           (d) line shapes Doppler+vdW (+Stark only >1e16, see section 4);
           (e) self-absorption/trapping on resonance columns (P2).
           Convolve with a declared instrument function.
 NOTES     This module is what turns the "camera for emissions" from a
           qualitative picture into a scored anchor, and it is the direct
           discriminator PLAN (44)/(38)/(37) keeps asking for (the high-F2
           fluorescence observable).  Line-list ingestion must follow the
           table-walk rule.

 M3  CAMERA / STREAK / SIDELIGHT IMAGING  (the camera)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MEASURES  (a) sidelight fluorescence waveform through a window (fast
           photodiode / streak: DE-AR's own gain proxy -- "248.5 nm
           fluorescence intensity is proportional to small-signal gain");
           (b) time-resolved 1-D streak image (slit x sweep);
           (c) gated 2-D images of the discharge / afterglow.
 HAVE      All the raw material, no projection step: REBUILD/MODULES/ase.py
           already carries the voxel ray machinery (Amanatides-Woo
           _segments, ray_intensity chord transfer, path_gain) but uses it
           only for ASE/parasitics; phantom11/spatial/sim_2d.py produces
           kT/P_IB/E-over-N 2-D field slices with nothing that images
           them; Argon_UI/emission.py computes "streak-planning" photon
           budgets (P_nir_W, P_vuv126_W, trapped resonance VUV) but no
           time-resolved instrument trace.  argon_laser/optimize.txt s10
           asserts "predicted n_e(t) and kT(t) directly overlay the streak
           data" -- today NO code converts state into a streak signal, and
           the streak sees LIGHT, not n_e.
 BUILD     imaging.py on top of M2's emissivity: line-of-sight integration
           over the device grid (reuse ase.py chords; REBUILD keeps only
           y_final spatially resolved -- add a save-times hook for the
           emissivity field), times window transmission, relay etendue
           (A*Omega), photocathode radiant sensitivity(lambda) from the
           streak datasheet, sweep-speed mapping and trigger-jitter
           convolution.  Deliverables: photodiode trace [A], streak image
           [counts(y,t)], gated frame [counts(x,y)], and the sidelight
           g0-proxy waveform scored against P_out(t).
 NOTES     Photocathode trap already flagged in new_item_list.txt s8: S-20
           dies ~850-900 nm, the Ar NIR lines sit at 750-912 nm -- the
           forward model should carry the QE curve so this shows up as a
           predicted signal level, not a surprise.  Far-field/divergence is
           deliberately NOT here (see M11).

 M4  ELECTRICAL WAVEFORMS AS THE SCOPE SEES THEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MEASURES  V(t) at the HV probe, I(t) at the Rogowski/CVR, dI/dt, ring
           period, reversal fraction, and the inferred L_loop -- the FIRST
           data the bench will ever produce (optimize.txt s9 measurement
           ladder step 1 is a dummy-load ring-down).
 HAVE      The physics exists, the observable does not: unified/drives.py
           EnergyConservingCircuit integrates V_cap(t) as a real ODE state
           and exposes current_A() (nothing plots either);
           light_unified/circuit.py + pfl.py carry a discharge circuit and
           an N-section Guillemin PFN with real source impedance; REBUILD
           circuit.py produces U_gap/I1/I2/E-over-N traces (returned,
           0-D mode only).  Argon_UI/circuit.py -- the one facing the
           actual bench -- is SCALAR algebra only (I_pk = V/R, ring period
           as a closed form; no time-domain solve).  CIRCUIT.txt predicts
           25-33 kA and 2.4-3.2 us ring by hand.  No spark gap, no probe
           response, no jitter anywhere (P5).
 BUILD     scope.py + a time-domain RLC solve for the argon driver (port
           light_unified/circuit.py rather than re-derive): states ->
           predicted probe records: CVR = R*I; Rogowski = M*dI/dt through
           the integrator droop RC from the PEM CWT datasheet; HV probe
           through its bandwidth (P6015A ~75 MHz); report ring period,
           reversal fraction (the HURON-lifetime damping-resistor decision
           hangs on it, new_item_list CIRCUIT addenda), and L_loop
           recovered from the ring exactly as the bench will recover it.
           Add plasma impedance Z(t) = V_gap/I as a first-class output
           (REBUILD computes conduction current inline and never returns
           sigma or Z).
 NOTES     Cheapest module on this list and the first one falsifiable with
           hardware already ordered.  Also the natural home for the
           spark-gap closure model (P5).

 M5  PRESSURE GAUGE + ACOUSTIC OBSERVABLES  (the pressure gauge)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MEASURES  (a) static fill (capacitance manometer -- trivial, an input);
           (b) the PROMPT pressure jump after a shot; (c) acoustic ringing
           of the chamber; (d) the slow decay back as heat hits the walls.
 HAVE      Nothing.  Pressure is an input everywhere; REBUILD freezes n_tot
           at t=0 even while integrating T_g as state; gas_temp.py and
           reprate.py explicitly neglect acoustics by timescale argument;
           plans.txt T3.10 plans "acoustic/rarefaction -> refractive index
           -> optical path" but nothing is built.
 BUILD     pressure.py: Delta-p = (gamma-1) * E_thermalized / V (gamma =
           5/3 monatomic) with E_thermalized read STRAIGHT FROM THE LEDGER
           (gas_thermal store) -- i.e. the pressure jump is ledger
           calorimetry, an independent closure check on deposited energy;
           acoustic arrival/ring from chamber geometry (c_s(Ar, 300 K) ~
           320 m/s -> first wall arrival ~0.5 ms, kHz-class modes);
           chemistry term (F2 -> 2F transient in KrF mixes; none in pure
           Ar); wall-conduction decay [s-class].  Output: p(t) at the
           gauge port at manometer bandwidth + an optional piezo waveform.
 NOTES     Magnitude check: even 20 J thermalized into the whole 35 L
           chamber gives ~380 Pa ~ 2.9 Torr -- fat against a 1000-Torr
           head's ~0.5 Torr floor; localized in a ~5 cm3 discharge channel
           it is tens of atm before mixing (the "loud" in the safety
           addenda).  This is the cheapest CALORIMETER the lab will own:
           it scores the ledger's E_dep without any optics.  Feeds P1.

 M6  CO2 PROBE AS A DIAGNOSTIC (TRANSMISSION + PHASE / INTERFEROMETRY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MEASURES  (a) transmitted 10.6 um energy/waveform through the plasma
           (pyro head / photon-drag detector, both on the addenda list);
           (b) probe phase shift = line-integrated n_e (interferometry);
           (c) beam deflection from gradients (schlieren-class).
 HAVE      The probe exists in the model ONLY as a heater: Argon_UI injects
           MTL-5 inverse-bremsstrahlung heating and predicts the Te reheat
           and the DR stall (the delay-scan discriminator, PLAN (67)/(68))
           -- but never predicts the probe's own absorbed fraction on the
           argon path.  The AIR twin has the one-liner (airkinetics.py
           f_abs = 1 - exp(-alpha*L)).  unified/optics.py ray-traces the
           10.6 um beam through n_e(x,y) with the critical-density contour
           -- for deposition, not as a measured trace.
 BUILD     Expose f_abs(t) on the argon path (the alpha_ib is already
           computed -- one line); add phase: delta-phi = (pi L / lambda) *
           (n_e / n_c) with n_c(10.6 um) = 9.8e18 cm-3, plus the NEUTRAL
           term (n-1 = 2.8e-4 at 1 atm Ar) with its opposite sign and its
           acoustic (us-class) time signature -- report both so a future
           two-color or time-gated measurement can split them; deflection
           angle from transverse gradients along the chord.
 NOTES     Sensitivity: n_e = 1e15 cm-3 over 10 cm -> ~3 rad at 10.6 um
           (easily fringe-countable); 1e13 -> 0.03 rad (needs care).  A 1%
           density rarefaction over the same path gives ~0.2 rad -- same
           order as small n_e signals: quoting both terms is what makes
           the module honest.  Zero new hardware: laser and detectors are
           already on the list.

 M7  ABSORPTION COLUMNS: F2 BURNUP, METASTABLE TDLAS, PER-ABSORBER ALPHA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MEASURES  (a) F2 column via its broadband UV absorption -- the shot-to-
           shot halogen burnup a transmission monitor sees; (b) Ar*/Kr*
           metastable column + gas temperature via a narrow diode probe on
           811.5 nm (TDLAS); (c) the 248 nm probe transmission exp[(g0 -
           alpha)L] -- the g0/alpha measurement itself, decomposed.
 HAVE      f2_audit.py ledgers F2 fate internally (flags ~99.7% burn in
           anchor-class runs; PLAN (28) says 30-99% depletion breaks the
           fixed-mix EEDF tables) but nothing converts it to a
           transmission trace.  The 248 nm absorber cross-section set
           (F-, F2, Kr*/Kr** photoionization...) already sits in
           unified/ebeam.py and in REBUILD network.py PHOTO_* -- but
           REBUILD folds every absorber into ONE aggregate r_x
           (chemistry.py) and never reports the split.  Argon_UI has the
           811.5 nm A-value and Voigt width already.
 BUILD     columns.py: per-species line-of-sight optical depths tau_i(t) =
           sigma_i * integral(n_i ds) for declared probe wavelengths;
           TDLAS profile (absorption lineshape -> T_gas from the width,
           column from the area -- sigma_0 at 811.5 nm is ~8e-13 cm2, so
           even 1e10 cm-3 metastables over 10 cm give tau ~ 0.08:
           exquisitely sensitive, THE afterglow instrument for the
           prototype); and the per-absorber breakdown of alpha_248(t).
 NOTES     The alpha decomposition is directly load-bearing NOW: the open
           REBUILD warn (g0/alpha 2.4-2.9 vs empirical 4-12, scorecard
           OMISSIONS) needs to know WHICH absorber is overweight, and the
           omitted ArKr+ manifold carries a 1.5e-17 cm2 photoabsorption
           that lands exactly there when it goes in (P3).

 M9  IN-AIR CALIBRATION OBSERVABLES  (first light on the bench)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MEASURES  The HV + streak calibration shots happen IN AIR (item_list:
           In-Air Calibration Experiment).  First light the streak sees is
           the N2 second-positive system (C->B, 337 nm), self-terminating;
           plus the acoustic crack of the channel.
 HAVE      airkinetics.py carries the Kossyi air afterglow + f_abs and the
           delay-scan discriminator; PLAN (68) explicitly scoped N2 337 nm
           OUT ("discharge-phase, self-terminating") -- correct for the
           afterglow physics, but it is exactly what the calibration shots
           will photograph.  The validated 1-atm air LoKI swarm tables
           (N2/O2/CO2) already exist in-tree.
 BUILD     n2_sps.py: N2(C) excitation from the air tables' rates during
           the current pulse -> 337 nm band waveform (ns-class,
           self-terminating) -> through M3's streak/photodiode response.
           Optionally the cylindrical blast-wave arrival time at a wall
           (energy-in-channel cross-check with M4/M5).
 NOTES     Small module, big leverage: it makes the ENTIRE calibration
           campaign quantitative (streak sweep speed, trigger delay, HV
           timing all get scored against a predicted waveform, not a
           guessed one) before a single argon fill.

 M10 MICROWAVE S-PARAMETERS (S11/S21) FOR THE DE-AR DIAGNOSTIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MEASURES  The program's named microwave diagnostic: S11/S21 transmission
           through the e-beam-pumped KrF plasma, expected alpha ~ 0.2 /cm
           at n_e 1e13 cm-3, 100 GHz (DE-AR_targets.txt).
 HAVE      ARPAE/pic3d already measures R/T/A fractions against a vacuum
           reference with a noise floor, inverts alpha from T, and carries
           the 100 GHz alpha anchor + far-field horn cuts.
           light_unified/microwave.py has alpha and n_crit.  What is
           missing is only the FORMAT the instrument speaks: swept-
           frequency S11(f)/S21(f) referenced to the waveguide ports, and
           the same quantities emitted from the DEVICE engines (REBUILD /
           spectre n_e(t) -> slab R/T/A(t)) so a network-analyzer trace
           can score a kinetics run, not just the PIC box.
 BUILD     sparams.py: thin wrapper -- slab/Drude R,T,A -> S11/S21 in dB
           vs f and vs t at fixed f, port-referenced, with the ARPAE
           anchor as its gate.
 NOTES     Mostly bookkeeping, and it discharges a literal DE-AR milestone
           (M2.6 quantifies the microwave-boost effect; sim<->experiment
           15%).

================================================================================
2.  OVERLOOKED PHYSICS / COMPONENTS EXPOSED BY THE SWEEP
================================================================================

 P1  PRESSURE/DENSITY FEEDBACK + Tg-DEPENDENT RATES.  REBUILD integrates
     T_g as state but freezes n_tot at t=0: no thermal expansion, no
     E/N drift from density, and quench rates are evaluated at fill
     temperature (PLAN (11) flagged Orestes' k28 ~ Tg^-3.9 scaling as
     unmodeled).  gas_dynamics.py / gas_temp.py / pic1d gas_heating carry
     the pieces (rarefaction factor, const-P/const-V EOS) -- none of it
     reached REBUILD.  Feeds M5, M6, M11.

 P2  RADIATION TRAPPING IN REBUILD.  Holstein imprisonment exists in
     light_unified/light_chem.py (Ar 106 nm) and Argon_UI (resonance VUV
     photon budget), but REBUILD's VUV radiative rows (KR2_RAD 147 nm,
     AR2_RAD 126 nm, R39) escape freely -- overcounts radiative loss and
     miscolors M8.  Port the escape-factor treatment with citations.

 P3  PER-CHANNEL PHOTON-FATE LEDGER (and the ArKr+ manifold's absorber).
     Photodetachment (F- + hv) and excited-state photoionization ARE in
     the networks -- but REBUILD aggregates all absorbers into one r_x and
     the ledger books one radiated_escape scalar.  Expose per-channel
     photon fates (who absorbed, who emitted, at what wavelength): it is
     free bookkeeping at RHS-assembly level, it feeds M2/M7 directly, and
     it is where the omitted ArKr+ photoabsorption (1.5e-17 cm2, scorecard
     OMISSIONS, leading suspect for the x0.60 Kannari warn) will land.

 P4  HIBACHI / FOIL PHYSICS.  The empty HIBACHI/ directory at repo root is
     the tell.  REBUILD reduces the foil to a scalar transmission
     (beam.py); unified/ebeam.py already carries multi-pass geometry
     (sides, backscatter, foil transmission) that never migrated; the
     Johnson-Hunter x2.4 thin-cell backscatter enhancement is explicitly
     unmodeled (PLAN 2026-07-01 entry).  Missing: foil energy deposition /
     heating limit, rib shadowing (-> deposition stripes a camera SEES --
     ties to M3), pressure-foil loading.  This is the component an e-beam
     KrF device lives or dies by (Electra's hibachi is half the
     engineering).

 P5  PULSER + BREAKDOWN AS PHYSICS: spark-gap closure and jitter.  No
     spark gap model anywhere; the first-principles circuit "does not
     break down" (PLAN (41)/(39) carried limitation); jitter appears in no
     module.  The gap's time-varying channel resistance (Rompe-Weizel /
     Braginskii class, cited) is the source impedance the M4 waveforms
     need, and formative-lag statistics are what the streak's trigger
     scatter will measure.  gui_v2's "no simulated pulser BY SCOPE" was a
     0-D-cell decision -- the bench makes it load-bearing.

 P6  MAGNETIC / EM FIELDS.  "[TODO] EM / magnetic fields -- electrostatic
     only everywhere" (plans.txt).  Matters twice: e-beam guide field
     (Electra-class transport) and the ARPAE EM instrument is the only EM
     code in the tree.  Known scope decision -- recorded here because the
     e-beam mode's deposition symmetry quietly assumes it.

 P7  IMPURITY / WALL / WINDOW CHEMISTRY.  No impurity species exists in
     any network (HF appears only as a heat-capacity table entry); no
     H2O/O2/N2-in-Ar quenching, no HF formation, no wall F loss /
     recombination beyond ambipolar diffusion, no electrode sputtering, no
     window color-center/damage model even though ase3d already computes
     window fluence J/cm2 (compare against cited damage thresholds -- one
     line).  The new_item_list gas-handling section literally asks
     "(OPTIONAL) inert-gas purifier if impurity quenching matters to the
     kinetics" -- an impurity tier answers that purchasing question, and
     the air-vs-argon discriminator scan is already its first anchor.

 P8  ELECTRON-ELECTRON COLLISIONS / CR CLOSURE.  Already on the boards
     (REBUILD scorecard omission; T1.2 cold-Te CR balance; two-region
     open item 4) -- repeated here only because it changes M1's predicted
     Thomson spectrum (e-e Maxwellianizes the tail) and M2's line ratios.

 P9  PIC-SIDE DEVICE GAPS (pointer).  PIC_MCC_3D.txt s2.1's own nine: ion-
     neutral collisions/charge exchange, electrodes + external circuit +
     surface charge, secondary emission, Coulomb collisions, implicit
     mover, merge/split, foil injection; README_LIMITS s2.2 isotropic-
     scattering EEDF bias (~10x hot tail).  Tracked there; not duplicated.

 P10 UNCERTAINTY PROPAGATION INTO OBSERVABLES.  EST-row brackets (x1/3..
     x3) already sweep g0/eta in REBUILD -- extend the same sweep through
     the M-modules so every synthetic signal carries an error band.
     DE-AR's 10%/15% agreement milestones are meaningless without it, and
     it is the predictive-only answer to "how wrong could the trace be".

================================================================================
4.  CONSIDERED AND DISMISSED (so they are not re-derived later)
================================================================================

  - LANGMUIR PROBES at 1 atm: collisional sheaths um-thick, probe theory
    invalid mid-pulse, arc hazard on a 15 kV bench.  The afterglow
    electron density is better served by M6 (CO2 phase/absorption) and,
    later, a microwave hairpin/cutoff probe (M10 machinery).
  - STARK-BROADENING n_e below ~1e16 cm-3: at 1 atm the vdW width
    (~15 GHz ~ 0.03 nm at 811 nm) buries Stark widths for n_e <= 1e15.
    Keep as an M2 by-product above 1e16, never a primary diagnostic.
    (Seeding H2 for H-beta would poison the purity the prototype exists
    to test.)
  - EMITTED BREMSSTRAHLUNG / X-RAY dose on the 15 kV bench: endpoint
    15 keV, thin windows, negligible signal -- and REBUILD's beam.py
    already documents <1% loss below 1 MeV.  Revisit only for a real
    600 kV e-beam machine (then it is the standard beam-uniformity
    monitor).
  - IN-CELL SRS/SBS/filamentation at MW/cm2-class intracavity intensity
    and 1 atm: far below thresholds; plans.txt already parks it.  The
    real optical-path physics at rep rate is thermal (T3.10 -> M11/P1).
    (External SBS phase-conjugate cells are a different project.)
